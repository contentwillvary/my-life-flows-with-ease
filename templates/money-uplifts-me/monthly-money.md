**What £100 a month is worth:** saved and invested at 5% after inflation, £100 a month grows to about £15,500 in 10 years and £41,000 in 20. Price-tag your spending, don't cut joy blindly.

- **Order:** pay every minimum, then put extra on the highest interest rate first (cheapest overall), or the smallest balance first if quick wins keep you going.
- **0% deals:** a balance transfer or 0% purchase card can stop interest while you clear it; note when the 0% ends in the Calendar.
- **Student loan:** usually behaves more like a tax than a debt (repaid as a % of income, written off after a set number of years). Don't overpay without checking whether you'd ever clear it.
- **Struggling?** Free, confidential help: StepChange, National Debtline, Citizens Advice.
