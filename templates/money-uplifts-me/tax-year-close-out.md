# Tax year close-out

[← Money uplifts me](README.md)

Once a year, around April. Tick what applies.

- [ ] Use what's left of this year's ISA allowance before 5 April (£20,000 across all ISAs; Lifetime ISA up to £4,000 of it)
- [ ] Pension top-up if it makes sense (tax relief at your highest rate)
- [ ] Employed: save your P60; check next year's tax code
- [ ] Self-employed: gather income and expenses; file Self Assessment by 31 January; note payments on account
- [ ] Company director: send the year's records to your accountant; plan salary and dividends for the new year
- [ ] Side income: check whether it's over the £1,000 trading or property allowance
- [ ] Claim tax relief on work costs you paid yourself (e.g. professional fees, uniforms, working from home if eligible)
- [ ] Record the year's wins and your net worth in the Wins log
