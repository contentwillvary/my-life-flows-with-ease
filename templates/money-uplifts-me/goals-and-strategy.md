## Milestones

## Safety net

- **Target:** 3–6 months of essentials in an easy-access saver (example: £1,800 × 3 = £5,400). More if your income varies or you're self-employed.
- **Yours:** *\[current\]* of *\[target\]*.
- Keep it separate from everyday money so it isn't spent by accident.

## Saving and investing: a sensible order

1. **Workplace pension up to the full employer match:** free money (example: 5% from you plus 3% from the employer).
2. **Clear high-interest debt** (credit cards, overdrafts, buy now pay later once interest starts).
3. **Build the safety net.**
4. **Lifetime ISA if buying a first home** (25% government bonus, up to £4,000 a year, ages 18–39 to open).
5. **Stocks and shares ISA** for goals 5+ years away (up to £20,000 a year across all ISAs; gains tax-free).
6. **More pension:** tax relief at your highest rate; locked until your pension age (57 from 2028).

Keep it simple: one low-cost global index fund often does the job. If values matter, ethical, ESG or faith-based funds exist too; check what they actually exclude.
