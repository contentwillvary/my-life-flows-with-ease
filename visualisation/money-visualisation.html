    What the three milestones mean

    Three ways to see how far your savings and investments could carry you. They come from an established planning approach called FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early). Here they're reference points for how secure you are, not a plan or an assumption about how you'll work.

    • Retirement covered (in FIRE terms, Coast FIRE): what you've built up would grow on its own to fund retirement at the age you plan for, even if you saved nothing more.
    • Lower income covered (Barista FIRE): if your take-home dropped to the amount you set (a pay cut, fewer hours, time out for study or caring), your investments would make up the difference.
    • Financially independent (Full FIRE): your investments cover all your costs. Money outside your pension carries you to your pension access age; the pension and the State Pension take over after that.
    A planning aid, not financial advice.
