Write {{NAME}}'s weekly admin update in the admin doc ({{ADMIN_DOC}}). The aim is less stress: they should finish reading feeling on top of things.

About {{NAME}}: {{ABOUT}}

If "This Week" is already dated within the last 3 days, this is a repeat run: only add anything new since then, and reply with a one-line summary.

1. Read the whole admin doc, then search email from the last 8 days for appointments, bookings, deliveries, renewals, replies they're waiting on, and anything new that needs action. Ignore anything about money; that belongs in the money doc.
2. Update the **Everything at a glance** table:
   - Change Status, Next step and When for anything that moved. Mark finished steps with ✓.
   - Add new items in the right theme. One row per item.
   - Sort by urgency: 🔴 first, then 🟡, 🟢, ⚪. Within a status, soonest date first.
   - Use the status rules written under the table.
3. Add any new detail (references, dates, who said what) to the top of that theme's tab (the Theme cell links to it). A new theme gets its own tab and a linked row.
4. Move anything fully finished out of the table and into **Wins** (newest first, with the date).
5. Rewrite **TL;DR** and **This Week**: one win, what moved, what needs a decision, and at most three small actions. Set "Updated" under This Week to today.
6. Reply with a 3-line summary and the doc link.
